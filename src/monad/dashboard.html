Why it's safe to be the fee payer

Exact terms

The amount, recipient, token (EIP-712 domain), and chain are all checked against the seller's own quote, never the client's echo.

Single-use nonces

Each nonce is issued by the seller, bound to the resource and price, and burned before submission. EIP-3009 enforces it again on-chain.

Simulate first

Unfunded or invalid authorizations fail in simulation, so attackers can't make the seller burn gas.

Buyer policy

Budget, per-call cap, and payee allow-list are checked before signing. That's why the third report above gets refused.
